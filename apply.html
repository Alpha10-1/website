Course 2: Occupational Certificate In Child And Youth Worker

A professional qualification that equips individuals with the skills and knowledge needed to support children and young people in various social, educational, and community settings.

It prepares workers to provide care, guidance, and intervention for children facing challenges such as abuse, neglect, homelessness, substance abuse, and mental health issues.

Skills Covered:

  • Child Development & Psychology – Understanding emotional and cognitive growth.
  • Counseling & Conflict Resolution – Helping children and youth deal with trauma and challenges.
  • Family & Community Support – Working with families to create safe environments.
